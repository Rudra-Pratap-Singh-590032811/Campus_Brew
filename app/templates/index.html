Campus Brew Café

CS-2 · CAPSTONE PROJECT

Build it. Then break it.

A little café app made for our CS-2 project — with plenty of testing behind the scenes.

01 / THE PRODUCT

A café buddy in your browser

Responsive chat interface, searchable menu, weekly hours, conversation history stored locally in SQLite, and response feedback.

02 / THE SAFETY

Answers from our café info

Responses use local café data. Unverified questions are declined. Listed allergens are disclosed with a clear cross-contact warning.

03 / THE EVALUATION

Tried, tested, improved

Includes 12 documented adversarial questions, root-cause analysis, and automated regression tests. Read the docs folder in the submission.
